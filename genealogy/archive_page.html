A place for relatives to add what the written tree lacks: photographs, baptism or marriage certificates, obituaries, letters, and corrections. Everything added here is listed below for the whole family, tied to the person it concerns.

What helps most: any photo of a person in the tree (with who, where and when), any document with a date, and corrections to names or placements. Say where a photo came from so it can be credited. Please do not upload anything you would not want other family members to see.
