- Munasque (no ñ): ≈100 bearers; PH, also USA, Qatar, England. https://forebears.io/surnames/munasque
- Lapu-Lapu City surname-rank page (not opened): https://forebears.io/philippines/central-visayas/cebu/lapu-lapu

## Mactan surnames
- Umpad/Ompad: 4,084 bearers PH (25% Central Visayas). https://forebears.io/surnames/umpad . Wiktionary Appendix: Cebuano surnames lists Ompad no. 9 and Patalinghug no. 13 among most common in Lapu-Lapu City. https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Appendix:Cebuano_surnames
- Patalinghug: old Mactan family (Mangubat surname article) https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mangubat_(surname)
- Booc: Wiktionary lists under Mactan; https://forebears.io/surnames/booc
- Ynot ≈1,895 bearers https://forebears.io/surnames/ynot ; Etcuban ≈679 https://forebears.io/surnames/etcuban ; Cosip ≈704 https://forebears.io/surnames/cosip ; Gusat ≈51 (mostly Romania) https://forebears.io/surnames/gusat — Philippine form may be spelled differently; Pino: Spanish/Italian origin https://en.geneanet.org/surnames/Pino
- Verdict: Umpad/Ompad, Patalinghug, Booc are documented Mactan/Lapu-Lapu surnames; Ynot, Etcuban, Cosip are Philippine but unlocalised; Gusat and Pino not shown to be Mactan surnames.

## Record collections
- FamilySearch "Philippines, Central Visayas, Church Records, 1737–2005", coll. 5000324 (Bohol, Cebu, Negros Oriental, Siquijor; partially indexed; ~596,566 index records Sept 2026). https://www.familysearch.org/search/collection/5000324
- "Philippines, Catholic Church Records, 1520–2014", coll. 2861657. https://www.familysearch.org/search/collection/2861657
- FamilySearch Wiki inventory lists parish "Nuestra Señora de la Virgen de Regla, Lapu-Lapu City, Cebu" https://familysearch.org/wiki/en/Philippines,_Catholic_Church_Records,_1520-2014_-_Inventory ; Cebu wiki page https://www.familysearch.org/en/wiki/Cebu_Province,_Philippines_Genealogy
- Civil registration: Spanish Period 1706–1911 coll. 1935452; Archives Division 1902–1945 coll. 2018411; Local 1888–1986 coll. 1410394; Central Visayas 1914–1996 coll. 5000194; National 1945–1996 coll. 1852584.
- Lapu-Lapu FamilySearch Center: 2 Tumulak St, Lapu-Lapu City. https://locations.familysearch.org/en/ph/lapu-lapu-city/2-tumulak-st
- PSA: archive generally starts 1945; pre-1945 yields negative certification unless endorsed. https://foi.gov.ph/requests/birth-certificate-psa-933576770502 ; national copies Jan 1932–Feb 1945 destroyed in WWII, LCR copies may survive: https://cms-z-assets.familysearch.org/d2/bd/f25915764f2a96e231c3a5862b6a/syllabus-filipino-genealogy-101.pdf
- To get a Feb 1940 Opon death record: request Civil Registry Form 2A transcription from the Lapu-Lapu City Local Civil Registry Office; endorse to PSA (citizen's charter https://www.psa.gov.ph/system/files/citizens-charter/CRS-CRSD-2.pdf); search FamilySearch 2018411/1410394 and the Opon parish burial register (5000324).
