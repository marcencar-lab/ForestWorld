## Middle-name convention
- FamilySearch "Filipino Genealogy 101": "prior to U.S. Colonization, Filipinos followed Spanish style. Afterward, mother's maiden name became all children's middle name." https://cms-z-assets.familysearch.org/d2/bd/f25915764f2a96e231c3a5862b6a/syllabus-filipino-genealogy-101.pdf
- Wikipedia "Filipino name": middle name "is usually, though not always, the mother's maiden name"; practice changed under US rule in the early 20th century. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filipino_name

## Age at marriage
- Singulate mean age at marriage for women: 21.1 (1903), 22.2 (1948), 22.8 (1970). https://catalog.ihsn.org/citations/46129
- Cagayan de Oro median female age at first marriage 19.1 (1942) to 21.6 (1962). https://pssc.org.ph/wp-content/pssc-archives/Philippine%20Sociological%20Review/1968/Num%203-4/20_Late%20Marriage_%20Abstract.pdf

- Implication: a man whose first known child was born in 1857 most plausibly married c. 1853–1856 aged 22–30 → born c. 1825–1835 (c. 1820 possible).
