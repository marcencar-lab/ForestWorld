## Opon / Lapu-Lapu City
- Augustinians (re)founded Opon as a Christian town 1730; ceded to Jesuits 1737, later returned. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lapu-Lapu_City . Cavada's Historia de Filipinas (quoted in Philstar) gives 1711 for the creation of "Opong" parish. https://www.philstar.com/the-freeman/opinion/2017/11/24/1762313/vicariate-southern-cebu-part-i/amp/ . Devotion to Nuestra Señora de la Regla attributed to first Augustinian parish priest Francisco Avalle, 1735 (travel blog, unconfirmed). https://benjielayug.com/2022/04/22
- First municipal election 1905 (Pascual dela Serna). US control of Cebu restored 1901.
- Population (Wikidata Q574903 citing censuses): 1903: 14,851; 1918: 20,988; 1939: 33,426; 1948: 37,280; 1960: 48,546. https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q574903 . Cebu province 653,727 (1903), 855,065 (1918) https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q13786 . 1877/1887 Opon figures not found; southern-Cebu parishes 164,046 (1867–68), 234,837 (1884–85) https://www.philstar.com/opinion/2020/01/24/1987349/cebus-population-1880s
- Economy: poor farmland; fishing, carving, guitar-making, boat-building. https://lapulapucity.gov.ph/articles/20 ; https://region7.dilg.gov.ph/lgus/lapu-lapu-city/ ; coconut/fishing https://www.britannica.com/print/article/330417 . Oil tanks at Opon drew Japanese air raids Dec 1941; old church damaged in WWII. https://www.ortigasfoundationlibrary.com.ph/collections/image-bank-database/opon-cebu
- Cityhood: RA 3134, 17 June 1961; inaugurated 31 Dec 1961; first mayor Mariano Dimataga. https://batasnatin.com/explainers/ra-3134-charter-of-lapu-lapu-city-explained-the-birth-of-the-city . Mactan airport 1966.
- Mactan–Mandaue Bridge: construction began 1970; inaugurated 4 July 1973. https://structurae.net/en/structures/mactan-mandaue-bridge ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mactan%E2%80%93Mandaue_Bridge . Before the bridge, Opon reached by ferry.
- Claveria decree 21 Nov 1849; Catálogo c. 61,000 names https://opinion.inquirer.net/127676/how-filipinos-got-their-surnames/amp ; standardized names appear in records from March 1850 (FamilySearch syllabus).

## Life expectancy and naming
- Life expectancy at birth ~31 (1870), >41 by 1940 (Statista, unsourced); UN 53.6 (1950); World Bank 59.2 (1960).
- Naming: after US colonisation the mother's maiden surname became the child's middle name https://cms-z-assets.familysearch.org/d2/bd/f25915764f2a96e231c3a5862b6a/syllabus-filipino-genealogy-101.pdf ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filipino_middle_names
